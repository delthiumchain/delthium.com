Overview

Delthium is an independent Layer-1 blockchain project focused on security, scalability, and long-term sustainability for real-world decentralized applications. The protocol emphasizes correctness, transparency, and conservative engineering over short-term hype.

Core Principles

  • Security First — Auditable, conservative protocol design
  • Performance — Efficient and scalable architecture
  • Decentralization — Validator-driven governance
  • Long-Term Vision — Sustainable real-world adoption

Roadmap

Phase 1 — Foundation

Core protocol architecture, consensus research, chain design, website launch, and internal testing.

Phase 2 — Public Testnet

Open testnet launch, validator onboarding, stress testing, and community feedback cycles.

Phase 3 — Mainnet

Mainnet deployment, tooling, documentation, and ecosystem development for builders.

Transparency & Independence

Delthium is developed independently. There are no public token sales, no financial guarantees, and no speculative promises. The project grows through technical progress, community feedback, and open development.
